Day Clock Privacy Policy

Day Clock uses Google Photos Picker only when you press the Photos button and sign in. You choose which photos to share.

The app uses the selected photos only to display them on your device. It does not upload, sell, or share your photos with anyone else, and it does not store them on a server.

To stop access, close the picker or revoke Day Clock’s access from your Google Account security settings.
